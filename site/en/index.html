Who we are

Beach Halle Genève is a beach volleyball club founded in 2023 by Geneva players and affiliated with Swiss Volley Région Genève. We are a non-profit association, and every franc we earn goes back into the school, the tournaments and the hall project.

In summer we run official tournaments and a school for every level at the Evaux, Vessy and Varembé. The rest of the year we work on what Geneva is still missing, a hall where people can play on sand whatever the season.

The coaches

Seven coaches ran the school in 2026, at the Evaux, Vessy and Varembé.

Georgiy Nasibullin

Coach, strength and conditioning

After sixteen years of professional volleyball in Europe and a master’s in sports science, he worked as strength and conditioning coach for Volero Zürich, Chênois and Infomaniak Genève Volley. Today he also works with Swiss fencers and an Olympic medallist in kayak.

Antoine Zaugg

Coach

Antoine is a regional beach volleyball coach in Geneva and has spent most of his career with juniors. He coached three seasons at Volley Düdingen, winning two league silver medals, then one season at Cheseaux. The high point so far is the gold medal with Geneva’s under-17 team.

Manda Randriatsimihoatra

Coach, strength and conditioning

As strength and conditioning coach at Infomaniak Genève Volley, Manda spends a lot of time on positioning, footwork and clean touches, because those are the basics you pay for later if you skip them.

Stefano Bianco

Coach

Stefano is the one who started beach volleyball at the Evaux in 2015, with the first training sessions and the first tournaments. He has been Geneva mixed champion twice and has also coached U15 juniors and women’s teams in the second league.

Bryan Biderbost

Coach, vice-president

He coached beginners at the Franchises for three seasons and led several junior camps for Swiss Volley Région Genève. In 2025 he won at B1, B2 and B3 level, and he knows how to help someone who is starting from zero.

Dario Santandrea

Coach, president

After years of football, Dario discovered beach volleyball in 2010 and got into it quickly, to the point of joining camps in Europe with Olympic coaches. This season he took over several school groups whenever the team needed him.

Fabrizio Bontà

Guest coach · Italy

Beach volleyball coach in Rimini since 2009, qualified with the Italian federation. He came to Geneva in September 2026 for a training weekend with our players.

How to sign up for a tournamentLicence, team and registration on VolleyManager

Everything happens on VolleyManager, the Swiss Volley platform, which is not very intuitive the first time you use it.

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Go to volleymanager.volleyball.ch and log in. If you don’t have an account yet, signing up takes a few minutes and your username will be firstname_lastname.

  2. 2

    Get a licence

    In the MyBeach module, order a category B licence. It costs 30 francs for the whole season, and you can pay by TWINT, card, PayPal or PostFinance.

  3. 3

    Form your team

    Send a request to your partner from VolleyManager, and the team exists as soon as they accept. Both players need a licence before the team can be created.

  4. 4

    Pick a tournament

    Filtering by region and category shows the tournaments that are still open. For a first tournament, B3 is the right level to start with.

  5. 5

    Register

    From your team’s page, sign up for the tournament you chose. Until the deadline you can withdraw at no cost, and after that only with a medical certificate.

Good to knowAccess, opening times and small details

Franchises · The snack bar is open all summer, and there are tournaments at weekends.

Evaux · The courts are only free when nobody has booked them. The restaurant is three minutes away.

Vessy · Free and open to everyone. The courts are sometimes taken down for other activities, and the sports centre website says when.

Varembé · Free, open to everyone, with lights until 9:30 pm.

Sous-Moulin · Open from May to September.

Vaudagne · Free and open to everyone.

La Bécassière · Free and open to everyone, but there are no lines, so bring your own.

Bellevue · The sand is a bit hard.

Coppet · It gets busy fast as soon as the sun comes out.

Pools, beaches and further afieldPaid entry and around the lake

With a paid entry, you’ll find one court at Genève-Plage in Cologny, another at Le Lignon in Vernier, two at Les Vergers in Meyrin, and beach volleyball next to beach soccer at Marignac in Lancy.

A little further away there are the Grande Jetée and Colovray in Nyon, then Gland, Crans, Morges and Vidy in Lausanne.

Coming without a partner« La gagne » and WhatsApp groups

At the Franchises and in Vessy, ask for « la gagne ». You’ll play against the team that wins the current game, with a teammate you find on the spot, and when it gets busy people start games in parallel so nobody waits on the sideline.

Some courts have their own WhatsApp group for setting up sessions. Ask around on site, or write to us at contact@bhge.ch or on Instagram, and we’ll put you in touch.

A beach hall for Geneva

In Lausanne, Bern, Basel and Zurich you can already play beach volleyball all year round. We want Geneva to have its own hall too, and we are discussing it with the City of Geneva.

With the support of Swiss Volley, Swiss Volley Région Genève, Genève Chênois Volley and Beach-Volley Club Vernier Genève.

Want to support the club in 2027?Three partnership packages

We offer three packages, starting at CHF 500.

Bronze

Your logo on our website, one Instagram post and a mention during a tournament or a class. When the BHGE jerseys come out, we name you along with the other partners.

Silver

Your logo featured on the website and on our posters and visuals, two to four Instagram posts and the title of official partner of Beach Halle Genève. We also talk about you during classes and events.

Gold

Your logo first on the website and on everything made for the project you support, a banner or beach flag by the courts, and four to six posts, one of them entirely about you. Where it’s feasible your logo goes on our jerseys, and we can work out an offer for our members together.

You can also support us in kind, the way Da Michele does with vouchers for tournament winners and AION-Sport with its video analysis software. Write to contact@bhge.ch or message us on Instagram, and the committee will be glad to walk you through the packages and amounts.
